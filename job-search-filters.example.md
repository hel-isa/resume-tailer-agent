# Job Search Filters (EXAMPLE)

> Copy to `job-search-filters.md`. These drive both proactive sourcing and the hard-disqualifier gate. Tune them to your real strengths and gaps so the agent stops surfacing dead-end roles.

## Target titles
- Title A
- Title B
- Title C

## Boolean search strings (paste into a job board)
- ("Title A" OR "Title B") AND (keyword OR keyword) AND (remote OR "your city")

## Green-list signals (fast-track when no hard blocker)
- Roles centered on your strongest, proven-winning patterns.
- Roles on your native tech stack and in your target industry or cities.

## Hard disqualifiers (fast-reject in one line)
List the blockers that historically caused most of your skips, so they are rejected before scoring. Examples of the shape:
- A specialization that is not yours as the role core.
- A required tool or platform you lack that is the spine of the role.
- A seniority bar in years above your real experience.
- A named language you do not have listed as required.
- An ownership level (for example architect) above your current scope.

## Notes
Review this list periodically against real outcomes. If a class of role keeps rejecting you at the applicant-tracking stage, add its distinguishing keyword here so you stop applying to it.
